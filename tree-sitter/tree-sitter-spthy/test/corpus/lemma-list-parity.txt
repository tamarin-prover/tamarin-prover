Empty attribute list cannot contain a comma

==================================================

theory Test begin

end

Empty output list cannot contain a comma
:error
==================================================

==================================================
Attribute lists reject consecutive commas
:error
==================================================

theory Test begin
lemma L [reuse,,]: "T"
end

---

Output lists reject consecutive commas
:error
==================================================

lemma L [output=[spthy,,]]: "T"
end
